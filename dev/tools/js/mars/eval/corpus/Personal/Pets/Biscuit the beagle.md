# Biscuit the beagle

Biscuit joined the family in April. He is two years old, weighs 11 kg,
and has opinions about the mail carrier.

## Feeding

| Time | Food | Amount |
|------|------|--------|
| 07:30 | Dry kibble | 90 g |
| 18:00 | Dry kibble with a spoon of wet food | 90 g |

No grapes, raisins, onions, or chocolate. Treats count toward the daily amount.

## Vet visits

- **May 14**: first checkup, rabies and distemper shots, microchip registered.
- **August 2**: ear infection in the left ear; drops twice a day for a week.
- **Next**: annual booster and a dental check in April.

## Walks and training

Two walks a day, about 40 minutes each. He pulls on the lead when he smells rabbits, so
we practice "heel" with treats in the quiet park first. He knows sit, down, wait, and
"leave it" for food on the pavement.

## Boarding

When we travel, Biscuit stays with the Moreno family three doors down. Pack his food, the
ear drops, his blanket, and the vet's number.
