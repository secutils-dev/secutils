# Tomato log

- **March 20**: sowed six varieties indoors on the windowsill.
- **April 28**: moved the seedlings to the greenhouse.
- **May 30**: planted out, 60 cm apart, with stakes.
- **July 4**: first ripe cherry tomatoes. Leaves curling on two plants: watering too unevenly.
- **August 15**: blight on the lower leaves of the beefsteak plants; cut them off and burned them.

Next year: water at the base in the morning, and mulch with straw.
