# Passport renewal

My passport expires in March, and some countries want six months of validity left, so I
renew it now.

## What to bring

- The current passport.
- Two recent photos, 35 by 45 mm, plain light background, no glasses.
- The birth certificate, original and a copy.
- Proof of address: a utility bill from the last three months.
- The application form, filled in but not signed until the clerk asks.

## Details for the form

- Full name exactly as on the birth certificate, including the middle name.
- Place of birth: city and country.
- Height and eye color.
- An emergency contact with a phone number.

Keep the old passport number at hand: the form asks for it. It is in the safe,
in the blue folder with the other identity papers.

## Fees and timing

The standard service costs 85 and takes about three weeks. The fast service costs 150 and
takes five working days. Pay by card at the office.
