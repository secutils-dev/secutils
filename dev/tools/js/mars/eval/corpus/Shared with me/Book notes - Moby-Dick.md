# Book club notes: Moby-Dick

Herman Melville, 1851. We read it over two months.

## What it is about

Ishmael signs on to the whaling ship Pequod. Its captain, Ahab, lost a leg to a white sperm
whale and steers the whole crew into a hunt for revenge across the oceans.

## Themes we argued about

- Obsession: Ahab sees the whale as the face of everything that has hurt him.
- Fate and free will: Starbuck can stop Ahab and chooses not to.
- The chapters on whale anatomy and rope-making: half the club loved them, half skipped them.

## Favorite line

"Call me Ishmael." Everyone picked a different one, so we kept the first.
