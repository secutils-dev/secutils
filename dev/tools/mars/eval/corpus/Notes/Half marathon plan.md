# Half marathon plan

Race day is October 18. Goal: under 1 hour 55 minutes, about 5:25 per kilometer.

| Week | Easy runs | Long run | Workout |
|------|-----------|----------|---------|
| 1 | 2 × 6 km | 12 km | 6 × 400 m fast |
| 2 | 2 × 6 km | 14 km | 3 × 1.5 km at race pace |
| 3 | 2 × 7 km | 16 km | 30 min tempo |
| 4 | 2 × 5 km | 10 km | rest week |
| 5 | 2 × 8 km | 18 km | 4 × 2 km at race pace |
| 6 | 2 × 8 km | 20 km | 40 min tempo |
| 7 | 2 × 6 km | 14 km | 5 × 1 km fast |
| 8 | 2 × 4 km | race | taper |

## Notes

- Easy means a pace where talking is comfortable.
- Practice drinking from a cup and taking gels on the long runs.
- New shoes at least three weeks before the race, never on race day.
