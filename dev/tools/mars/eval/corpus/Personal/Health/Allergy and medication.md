# Allergy and medication notes

For the family doctor and for anyone who looks after the kids.

## Allergies

- **Mia**: peanuts, severe. She carries an adrenaline auto-injector in her school bag.
- **Leo**: hay fever from May to July; a daily antihistamine helps.

## If Mia has a reaction

1. Use the auto-injector on the outer thigh, through clothes if needed.
2. Call the emergency number and say "anaphylaxis".
3. Lay her down with legs raised, unless she struggles to breathe; then let her sit.
4. A second injector after five minutes if there is no improvement.

## Repeat prescriptions

The antihistamine and the spare injectors are renewed every six months at the pharmacy on
the corner. The injectors expire after about a year: check the date in September.
