# Buttermilk pancakes

Makes about twelve, enough for four people.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Flour | 250 g |
| Sugar | 2 tablespoons |
| Baking powder | 2 teaspoons |
| Baking soda | 1 teaspoon |
| Salt | a pinch |
| Buttermilk | 500 ml |
| Eggs | 2 |
| Melted butter | 40 g |

## Method

1. Whisk the dry ingredients in a large bowl.
2. Whisk the buttermilk, eggs, and butter in a jug.
3. Pour the wet mix into the dry mix and stir until just combined. Lumps are fine.
4. Rest the batter for ten minutes while the pan heats.
5. Cook on a medium pan, a ladle at a time, until bubbles form, then flip once.

Serve with maple syrup and berries. The batter keeps for a day in the fridge.
